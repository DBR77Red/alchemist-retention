Which game archetypes should the 3–5 challenges cover?

Each archetype becomes a challenge with its own brief, target slider ranges, and grading thresholds. Select 3–5.

HC

Hypercasual

Mass installs, razor-thin sessions, almost pure ad monetization. Low retention is expected — revenue comes from volume. The opening challenge.

Win condition flavor: "D1 ≥ 35%, Ad Revenue ≥ $X, ARPU > $0.08"

IDLE

Idle / Incremental

Long sessions, slow dopamine drip, strong daily bonus dependency. Good D7/D30 is the goal. Light IAP, low frustration tolerance.

Win condition flavor: "D30 ≥ 8%, LTV ≥ $0.80, frustration events < threshold"

MID

Mid-Core RPG

Energy gates, loot systems, moderate IAP conversion. Needs a careful balance of frustration and reward. The "difficult" challenge — many dials to tune.

Win condition flavor: "D7 ≥ 18%, ARPPU ≥ $8, conversion ≥ 2%"

GTCH

Gacha / Collectible

Loot frequency is the core loop. High ARPPU from whales, low overall conversion. Retention lives and dies on the dopamine spike cadence.

Win condition flavor: "Loot spikes ≥ X, ARPPU ≥ $15, D30 ≥ 5%"

PZL

Casual Puzzle

Ad-heavy, low IAP, very high D1 expectations. Energy cost and ad frequency tension is the core design challenge. Broad audience, low tolerance for friction.

Win condition flavor: "D1 ≥ 45%, Ad Revenue ≥ $X, IAP conv ≥ 1%"

SUB

Subscription / Premium

Zero ads, zero energy walls, high shop price. Revenue comes entirely from a paying base. Retention is everything — churned players don't renew.

Win condition flavor: "D30 ≥ 12%, LTV ≥ $4, ad frequency = 0"

Pick 3–5. The order you select roughly becomes the difficulty progression.
