Which revenue metrics should the simulation track?

These become both the grading dimensions and the new dashboard metrics. Select all that feel essential.

$

ARPU — Avg Revenue Per User

Total revenue ÷ total players. The standard top-line mobile metric. Combines IAP and ad revenue into one number.

Industry benchmark: $0.05–$3.00 depending on genre

$+

ARPPU — Avg Revenue Per Paying User

Revenue ÷ players who actually spent. Shows how much your paying cohort spends, independent of conversion rate.

Industry benchmark: $5–$25 for mid-core games

LTV

LTV — Lifetime Value

Average revenue generated per player before they churn. The most important metric for UA spend decisions.

LTV > CPI = profitable growth

%

IAP Conversion Rate

% of players who made at least one purchase. Low conversion + high ARPPU = whale game. High conversion + low ARPPU = mass-market.

Industry range: 0.5%–5% typical

AD

Ad Revenue

Total revenue from ad impressions across retained players. Driven by ad frequency × retained cohort × eCPM slider.

Key for hypercasual and hybrid monetization
